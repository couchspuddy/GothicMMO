# The Penitent — Class Kit Design Document
**Vigil — Class Design Document**
*Status: Locked — Full Kit Designed, Zero Implementation*

---

## Identity Statement

The inward turn. The Penitent looked at what the Bleed was doing to humanity and turned inward rather than outward — not from weakness, since the outward response was already being provided by other factions, but because something essential was being lost in the survival and the wall-building that nobody was accounting for.

**Core action, in a single sentence:** *The Penitent watches and peers deep into the depths of the beings in front of them. Not just their allies, but the Accursed as well.*

They do not know what the Prior Flame is or why it exists within them. They attribute it to a calling — something felt, not seen or touched. They stand to gain and lose the most from battles with the Accursed, but more than their own stake, they do not wish to see their allies become subject to the thing they fight. In combat they are stoic and direct — they do not draw things out, not from survival instinct, not from tactical necessity, but from compassion. Their battles are fought through sadness, not necessity.

The Penitent does not diagnose. They do not know who an Accursed *was* in the analytical sense — that is the Academy's domain. They know only that the thing in front of them was human. That distinction is load-bearing for the entire kit (see Design History below).

---

## Temporal Identity — What Distinguishes the Penitent Mechanically

Each class occupies a distinct temporal relationship to combat, and this is the Penitent's core point of mechanical differentiation from the other two designed classes:

- **The Hunter** operates *ahead* of the fight — scans, plans, reads what's coming next.
- **The Warden** operates *in* the fight — entirely present-tense, reacting the instant something happens.
- **The Penitent** operates *after* — reflection, by definition, requires something to have already occurred.

This is not a weaker or slower version of the other two temporal modes. It is a third one, and it is the temporal register the Selah moment's pause already lives in — the Penitent's kit is built to operate specifically inside that "after the fact" space more than any other class's.

---

## The Creed

- **Remember** — to be human is to be spiritual. The Penitent Remembers for the Accursed too — every Accursed is a human from whom the spiritual was extracted by force.
- **Endure** — through interior depth. The trenches of willpower built through spiritual practice; faith as structural support, not comfort.
- **Repay** — through acknowledgment. The dead, human and Accursed alike, are seen, mourned, and released.

## Kit Overview

| Ability | Type | Core Function |
|---|---|---|
| Be At Peace | Passive | Binary state; activates when an enemy dies; consumed when an ability triggers its bonus effect |
| Here, Now, Present | Passive | Ally damage taken builds Prior Flame charge; grants or amplifies healing on abilities |
| Antecedent Blood Vial | Weapon | Thrown vial creates a zone Accursed reject and flee from; allies heal slowly in the zone if Be At Peace is active |
| Last Rites | Tactical | Heals a targeted ally (amplified by Be At Peace) or marks a targeted enemy as suffering — suffering enemies deal reduced damage, and are instantly executed with a cooldown reset if Be At Peace is active |
| Recede | Movement (respite) | Reduces threat generated by the Penitent; holds Be At Peace active for the ability's duration; ends early if the Penitent deals damage to an enemy |
| Grief Given Voice | Covenant | Cleanses debuffs, heals allies based on missing health, grants damage resistance; if Be At Peace is active, grants full healing, greater resistance, and revives revivable allies |

Six slots, matching the Hunter and Warden's structure in count: two passives, two actives, one respite/movement-equivalent slot, one Covenant. Slot *function* is intentionally not uniform across classes — see Design History below for why the movement slot specifically diverges from a literal dash for this class.

## Passive Abilities

### Be At Peace

**What it is:** A binary state. Turns on when an enemy is defeated. Consumed the moment any ability checking for it triggers its Be-At-Peace-active bonus effect, turning back off.

**Design intent:** This is the mechanical realization of "letting them fight for humanity once more, using the Penitent as a vessel" — but deliberately universal rather than categorized. The Penitent does not know or diagnose *what* was restored; they only register that something human, however briefly, was present at the moment of death. Every other ability in the kit either responds differently while this state is active, or exists specifically to reach it — Be At Peace functions as a hub the whole kit orbits, rather than being owned by any single "activator" ability.

---

### Here, Now, Present

**What it is:** As allies take damage, the Penitent gathers charge from their Prior Flame. This charge allows the Penitent's abilities to heal in addition to their other effects; abilities that already heal have that healing significantly increased.

**Design intent:** Healing is not a separate resource bar bolted onto the kit — it is an emergent property of abilities that already exist for other reasons, becoming available or amplified once enough ally suffering has been witnessed. This is the precise mechanical translation of "steeling themselves post-combat" and reflection: the Penitent's care isn't their primary action, it's what their primary actions *become* once sufficient witnessing has occurred.

## Active Abilities

### Antecedent Blood Vial — Weapon Ability

**What it does:** The Penitent smashes a vial of their own blood at their feet. Accursed reject the area as unsafe and attempt to flee it, lowering the Penitent's aggro within the zone. If Be At Peace is active, allies standing in the zone heal slowly for the duration.

**Design intent:** The first ability in the game to use the Antecedent's own blood — their essence, their humanity — as a physical, thrown resource. Mechanically expresses "Remember" (holding one's own essence as something real and usable) fused with compassion-not-necessity: the Penitent isn't repelling Accursed through force or threat, they're creating a zone that Accursed themselves reject because something genuinely, unblemished human is present there.

---

### Last Rites — Tactical Ability

**What it does:** Targeting an ally triggers a heal, increased if Be At Peace is active. Targeting an enemy marks them as **suffering** — suffering enemies deal reduced damage. If Be At Peace is active when a suffering enemy would otherwise die, they are instead immediately executed, and Last Rites' cooldown resets.

**Design intent:** The strongest single expression of "letting suffering end" in the kit. The execute is not available on demand — it requires Be At Peace to be active, meaning the Penitent's capacity to end an Accursed's suffering outright is earned by having recently witnessed another death, not something they can simply choose at will. The cooldown reset on execute creates real chain-kill potential, but only for a Penitent actively managing Be At Peace's uptime — a genuine tempo and timing skill expression built around maintaining the state, not just holding a button. The damage/execute effect should read presentationally as mercy — a finishing blow — rather than a conventional combat hit, consistent with the class's compassion-not-necessity framing.

## Respite Ability

### Recede

**What it does:** The Penitent looks inward, reducing threat generated toward themselves. While Recede is active, Be At Peace is held active for its full duration, rather than being consumed by a single use. Recede ends early if the Penitent deals damage to an enemy.

**Design intent — why this occupies the "movement" slot without being a movement ability.** Early drafts explored a directional dash for this slot, mirroring the Hunter's Lunge and Warden's Hold the Line. This was rejected: "receding inward" is definitionally incompatible with moving through space, and forcing the Penitent into a literal-movement mold for slot-parity with the other two classes would have contradicted the class's actual verb. The design principle established by this class specifically: uniform slot *count* across classes is required (six slots, matching input complexity), but uniform slot *function* is not — a class's identity should determine what a slot does, not the other way around.

**Design intent — the Be At Peace interaction.** Recede is not a duplicate path to the same job as Last Rites. Last Rites is a single, deliberate, consumable use of Be At Peace tied to a concrete action. Recede converts the same binary state from a one-shot consumable into a **sustained window** — any ability used while Recede is active can draw on Be At Peace without immediately spending it, as long as the window hasn't ended and no damage has been dealt to enemies. A Penitent who times Recede well can chain multiple Be-At-Peace-empowered actions off a single triggering death, rather than needing a fresh kill for each one. The "ends early on dealing damage" clause prevents this from becoming a passive infinite-uptime tool — genuinely stepping back from offense is the cost of holding the state open.

**Solo/support relevance:** functions as the Penitent's answer to needing to reduce threat mid-combat while repositioning to support allies — support-archetype classes generally require some capacity to become momentarily lower-priority to enemies, and Recede is this class's specific, thematically-consistent version of that need.

## Covenant Ability

### Grief Given Voice

**What it does:** The Penitent's Prior Flame recognizes all the suffering laid out before them. Base effect: cleanses all debuffs on allies, heals allies based on missing health, grants a period of damage resistance. If Be At Peace is active when cast: the Flame surges further, combining with the remembered will of restored humanity — allies instead receive full healing, greater damage resistance, and any revivable allies are revived outright.

**Design intent:** The Penitent's version of Repay — everything witnessed across the fight resolving simultaneously, for allies specifically (in contrast to the Hunter's Reckoning, which resolves outward against enemies, and the Warden's Wall Remains, which resolves as personal endurance). The Be-At-Peace-active version is not a flat numerical upgrade over the base version — reviving revivable allies is a categorically different effect, not a bigger version of the same one. This means whether the Penitent has recently witnessed a death when this Covenant is cast can be the literal difference between "the party is topped up" and "the party gets someone back," creating the same kind of save-it-for-the-right-moment raid tension The Wall Remains creates for the Warden, arrived at through a mechanically distinct route appropriate to this class's identity.

## Design History — The Shape-Keying Correction

An earlier draft of this kit tied Be At Peace's bonus effects, and Antecedent Blood Vial's circle effects, to the specific Shaped archetype of the Accursed that died — Feral producing one effect, Predatory another, and so on, keyed to "who they were" in life via their Shape classification.

This was deliberately reworked and simplified. Diagnosing an Accursed's category and producing a mechanically distinct, correct response *is* systematic, pattern-based analysis — which is explicitly the Academy's defining trait, not the Penitent's. The Penitent's stated epistemic position is precise and load-bearing: **they do not know who an Accursed was. They know only that they were human.** A passive whose output varies by archetype classification quietly smuggled Academic logic into the Penitent's signature ability, even though the individual Shape-effects designed during that process (Feral granting Vital Damage, Predatory granting health-per-second as a direct restoration of their lost generosity, Wraith granting some form of anti-isolation effect rather than "charm," which was rejected for reading as Predatory-coded control rather than Wraith-coded involuntary connection) were individually well-reasoned.

Be At Peace and Antecedent Blood Vial were both simplified to universal, non-categorized effects as a result. The Shape-keyed variants explored during design are recorded here for reference but are **not canon** — the simplified, universal versions in this document are final.

**Assembled's "who they were" was resolved in a later design session** (see Tone & Sensory Bible, Humanity Spectrum section) — Assembled emerged from people who were most audaciously individual and self-possessed, inverted by the Bleed into a desperate, ongoing need to disappear into a mass rather than be singularly seen. This resolution arrived after the Shape-keying correction described above, so it was never actually applied to any Penitent ability — Be At Peace and Antecedent Blood Vial remain universal/non-categorized by design regardless, per the Academic-coding concern this section describes. This note is retained for historical accuracy only.

## Kit-Internal Cohesion

Every active ability in this kit either responds differently while Be At Peace is active, or exists specifically as a path toward reaching that state — Antecedent Blood Vial and Last Rites both check for and consume it; Recede sustains it as a window rather than consuming it; Grief Given Voice's most powerful version depends on it. This is the most tightly interlocked of the three designed kits: Be At Peace functions as a genuine hub the entire active kit orbits, rather than any one ability being its sole "activator."

---

## Design Tenet Alignment

- **Respect the player's intelligence** — Be At Peace's binary on/off state, consumed predictably by triggering abilities, is fully legible; the player always knows whether the state is active and what spending it will cost them.
- **The Accursed are never monsters** — Be At Peace's universal (non-diagnostic) trigger is the mechanical enforcement of this tenet at the class-identity level: the Penitent's power does not require categorizing an Accursed, only recognizing that something human was present in them.
- **Every system serves the feeling** — the correction removing Shape-keying from Be At Peace was made specifically because the categorized version, while mechanically interesting, did not serve the Penitent's actual emotional and philosophical identity, even though it was well-designed on its own terms.
