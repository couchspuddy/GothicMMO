## Overview

Eagle's Landing is an abandoned city — Philadelphia, circa two hundred years post-Bleed — taken and held by the Accursed. The player enters on a contract with little to no forward knowledge of what they're walking into. The level is a narrative journey inward, from the city's edge to its center, each encounter a scene in a story the player is living.

**Total contract target:** ~10 minutes
**Traversal (no enemies):** 2-3 minutes
**Standard encounters:** 4-4.5 minutes (3 encounters)
**Boss fight:** ~3 minutes
**Selah moments:** ~5-10 seconds each, between encounters

**Entry point:** Wooded edge of the city
**Time of day:** Dusk
**Player knowledge on entry:** Little to none
**Forward knowledge:** A Bestial Lucid holds the city center. That's all.

---

## Level Narrative Arc

Every encounter asks a question the player answers with their body.

- **Encounter 1:** Can you handle this city?
- **Encounter 2:** Can you handle this city when it fights back?
- **Encounter 3:** Can you handle this city when it tries to break you?
- **Boss:** Can you finish what you started?

The Selah moments between each encounter are the commas in that sentence. The boss is the period.

## The Approach

The player steps out of the tree line into dusk. The city is silhouetted against a dying sky. Gas lamps unlit for years. The last light catching building tops while streets below are already in shadow. The Accursed don't need light. The player does.

The tree line gives every class a brief observation window before combat begins. Nobody is immediately in a fight — the city exists for two or three seconds before it reacts to them.

## Encounter 1 — The Edge of Town

### Scene
The player's first read of the city. An intersection occupied by Thralls — not patrolling, not guarding. There because the Bleed drew them there. Before the Thralls, before the intersection: a civilian body. No faction gear. No weapons. Someone who looked at the city and thought it might be safer than whatever they were running from. They arrived maybe a day ahead of the player. The Bleed doesn't discriminate and the city doesn't warn you.

The Thralls aren't near the body. They didn't kill this person in any conventional sense. The Bleed completed its work. The player hasn't fired a shot and already understands what Eagle's Landing is.

### Composition
- 5 Thralls
- Organic placement across the intersection — two near the civilian body drawn by residual essence, two further down the street in aimless Thrall movement, one partially obscured in a doorway
- The doorway Thrall rewards observation during the entry window; punishes inattention mid-fight

### Design Intent
- Communicate that the city is genuinely dangerous without being insurmountable
- Introduce the vital point system naturally — first Thrall has a readable vital, it shifts after damage, player figures it out without a tutorial prompt
- Establish the weight of each kill

### Selah Moment
The civilian body produces nothing — the Bleed completed its work here, no interruption, no residue. The asymmetry tells the player why they matter. They interrupted something. The civilian didn't get that.

Clean Selah moment after the Thralls. Full restock. The first breath the city allows.

## Encounter 2 — The Complication

### Scene
Deeper into the city. Streets narrowing as the grid tightens toward the center. A building collapses ahead — not triggered by the player, just a world that has been decaying for two hundred years and doesn't care who gets caught in it. The primary route is blocked. The collapse opens a Z-shaped route through the destroyed building to the left — a winding interior path the player commits to without seeing the end.

First time inside rather than on the street. Tight corridors, bad sightlines, dusk light coming through holes in the ceiling and walls. This is the Accursed's space.

### Ground Floor
3 Thralls in hiding. Not tactical — instinctive, the way an animal presses against a wall when something loud happens. Player entry triggers them. They come out of doorframes, from behind collapsed interior walls. Disorganized but sudden. Confined space means no clean sightlines. The player is reacting, not reading.

### The Staircase
The beat between beats. Footsteps. Creaking structure. Something above.

### Second Floor
1 Retained. Alone. At the window. It was there before the player entered the building. It watched them clear the intersection. It watched the collapse. It watched the ground floor fight. By the time the player reaches the second floor the Retained has had sixty seconds of observation time on them.

This is the inversion of the player's intended role — the player is supposed to be the one who observes before engaging. The Retained already did that. It doesn't ambush from behind. It's at the window, in the open, waiting. It knows the player is coming. It just doesn't care.

### Design Intent
- Environment as active participant — the collapse happens to the player, not for them
- Introduce the Retained's cold assessment in a space that amplifies the threat
- The Retained alone on the second floor deserves space — this is the first time the player feels genuinely studied by an enemy

### Selah Moment
The Retained was conscious. It knew what it was. The crystallization here carries the poke-through — somewhere in that Retained was a person who watched a building collapse and climbed to the second floor to get a better view. That instinct was human. The Bleed just inverted what they did with it.

Clean Selah moment. Full restock. The city gives it without interruption — this time.

## Encounter 3 — The Wild Card

### Scene
The grid opens into a wider plaza as the player approaches the city center. They can see further ahead than they have since the tree line. What they see is wrong — too many Thralls, too spread out, occupying the space like they've been there for months. This is the outer edge of the Bestial Lucid's territory. The density isn't random Bleed saturation. It's the outer ring of a predator's domain.

### Wave 1
8-10 Thralls. Extended fight. Resource heavy. Steadfast is being spent. The largest encounter the player has faced. They earn the clear.

### The Interrupted Selah
The Selah prompt appears. The player moves to collect. Collection begins — the crystallization initiates. Then from an unexpected direction, the second wave arrives. Not the way the player entered. Not the obvious approach. The player is caught mid-breath.

The prompt disappears. The fight resumes.

### Wave 2
4-5 Thralls. Smaller than Wave 1 but arriving at maximum disruption — mid-collection, player resource-depleted from Wave 1.

### The Collection
Second wave cleared. The prompt returns. The Selah held through the interruption — the crystallization waited. Collection completes. Full restock.

The silence after this collection should feel longer than the previous Selah moments. The player earned this one twice.

### Design Intent
- Largest enemy count in the level creates genuine pressure
- The interrupted Selah teaches the player that Eagle's Landing doesn't respect the rhythm they've built
- Carries into the boss fight as anxiety — is the next Selah moment safe?
- The Bestial Lucid's territorial saturation expressed through encounter density

## The Approach to City Hall

After Encounter 3, the Thralls stop. Not because they've been cleared — there are none here. This close to the Bestial Lucid, even Thralls don't linger. The Bleed at this proximity is too concentrated, too active. Lesser Accursed are displaced by it.

The silence is worse than the fighting.

Environmental tells on approach:
- Claw marks on stone at heights that don't make sense
- Windows darkened from the inside
- Grand entrance of City Hall structurally warped

The player understands before they cross the threshold: whatever is in here has been here long enough to change the building around itself.

Inside — the rotunda. It doesn't look like a rotunda anymore. Everything breakable has been broken. Everything that could be pulled down has been pulled down. Not nesting — dismantling. The player's Prior Flame registers something before their eyes find it.

Then it moves.

## Boss — The Bestial Lucid

### Who She Was
A builder. Commissioned by the Capitol. One of the finest craftspeople Eagle's Landing produced. Her father built walls and parapets. She exceeded him. Precise, controlled, disciplined — someone who imposed order on materials and made spaces safe. She helped build the walls meant to keep the Bleed out. She was taken by the Bleed gathering lumber for those same walls.

Her father told her: Remember, Endure, Repay. She didn't understand what he meant.

### The Inversion
The Bleed took the person who built Eagle's Landing's walls and made her the thing that unmade the city. Not a stronger, more architectural predator — the complete collapse of control. Pure destruction of the very things she used to create. No precision, no intention, no order.

The building the player fights her in is structurally compromised because she's been in it. The most structurally dangerous location in Eagle's Landing is the one she's claimed. The most structurally sound person in the city's history has been compulsively weakening it from the inside.

### Fight Duration
~3 minutes. Two phases split at roughly 90 seconds each.

### Phase 1 — ~90 Seconds
Fast and aggressive but readable. Vital point moving on both damage threshold and independent timer. Environmental destruction happening passively during the fight — she's breaking the rotunda around herself. Debris falling. Floor sections becoming unstable. The architecture failing in real time because she's in it.

### The Phase Shift — The Stillness
The vital point moves to its most aggressively protected location. The Bleed reorients — not the person surfacing, the extraction mechanism tightening its grip. A beat of absolute stillness. Then Phase 2.

### Phase 2 — ~90 Seconds
Faster. Vital shifting more frequently. The destruction instinct turns fully on the player. The compromised structure becomes instinctual — driving the player toward unstable floor sections. The fight space shrinks. The player should be close to losing before they find the turn. Steadfast likely depleted. Absolution possibly triggered. The Reckoning — if saved — is the turn.

### The Selah Moment
The heaviest in the level. The world holds it fully this time — no interruption, no second wave. The crystallization of someone who built the walls meant to keep the Bleed out, taken by the Bleed gathering lumber for those same walls.

Her father's words. The creed she didn't understand. The player standing in the building she helped build.
