# The Tone & Sensory Bible
**Vigil — Foundational Cross-Discipline Reference Document**
*Status: Living document — locked pairs below, additional pairs expected over time*

---

## Purpose of This Document

Every discipline-specific document (Environment Art Direction, and future Character Art, Music, Sound Design, VO Direction documents) answers "how do we build this correctly." This document answers a different, higher question: **what does Vigil feel like, and how do we know when something — however well-crafted — has drifted from that feeling even while technically following every structural rule elsewhere.**

This document sits above individual discipline docs the same way the North Star sentence sits above individual narrative decisions:

> *"This is a game about carrying weight you didn't choose, in a world that forgot how to hope, alongside people who haven't."*

That sentence anchors narrative decisions. This document exists to translate the same anchor into **sensory** terms — color, sound, pacing, silhouette, motion — so that art, audio, and animation disciplines have something as precise to check their work against as writers already have.

**Working method:** every pair in this document should be traceable back to something already true about Vigil's locked mechanics or lore — never invented fresh for art's sake alone. A pair that can't be traced to an existing system or story truth doesn't belong here; it belongs in a mood board, which is a different and less durable kind of document.

**Why pairs, not adjectives:** a single mood word ("atmospheric," "gritty," "melancholic") is not checkable — many different, even contradictory games could all claim the same word. A paired contrast — *"X, not Y"* — is checkable, because Y is always a plausible-sounding, genre-adjacent target that would be easy to drift toward without ever consciously choosing to. The second half of each pair is doing as much work as the first.

## Emotional Register Pairs

These govern how the player should *feel* — the emotional posture of the game moment to moment, independent of any single system.

### Haunting, Not Frightening

A world where things don't make sense and could change at any moment — but where preparation means readiness, not helplessness. This is a direct sensory translation of something already true at the mechanical level: The Read, the vital point system, and the Hunter's entire "observe before engaging" identity all say the same thing — *this world rewards attention and preparation over panic.* Frightening implies the player is a victim of what they don't understand. Haunting implies the wrongness is real, but readable, if you're paying attention. Art, audio, and encounter pacing should never work against this by making threats feel arbitrary or unreadable — even the worst things in Vigil should be, on some level, things a prepared mind can parse.

### Reverent, Not Sacred

The world treats death and loss with real, consistent weight (Remember) — but this is not a game about worship or destiny. The Ember Court made a desperate bargain out of necessity, not a holy covenant out of faith. The Prior Flame is a burden carried through will, not a blessing bestowed by grace. **Drift signal:** if art or music direction begins reaching for religious iconography, choir-and-organ musical language, or messianic framing of the Antecedents, that's drift from a world built on a negotiated deal between desperate parties, not a divine calling.

### Tragic, Not Pitying

Every Accursed was a person the world failed — but the game's job is to make the player feel the weight of that without reducing the Accursed to helpless victims with no remaining agency or dignity in what they became. The Bestial Lucid is tragic because she was a builder, specific and skilled and real — she is not tragic in a way that asks the player to feel sorry for a helpless creature. **Drift signal:** Accursed movement, vocalization, and death animation should carry specificity and a residue of dignity — never generic monster-whimper or pure-victim framing that flattens who they were into who they became.

### Significant, Not Celebrated

The player's arrival matters — the world responds, something shifts — but nothing in Vigil should visually or musically treat the player as a chosen-one savior arriving to fanfare. The correct shape of a major beat is *release*, not *climax*: the world exhales, it does not cheer. **Drift signal:** a swelling hero-theme, a triumphant sting, or any visual "arrival" language borrowed from chosen-one genre convention. If a moment builds toward a crescendo rather than a release of held tension, check it against this pair.

### Earned Quiet, Not Ambient Calm

The Selah moment's silence is the single most important five seconds in the game — a pause the world explicitly does not offer anywhere else. This only works if ambient sound design commits to **never fully resting** anywhere else in the game, even faintly. **Drift signal:** any other space in the game achieving a similarly restful, undisturbed quiet dilutes the one moment designed to own that effect entirely. If a "peaceful" area is ever this quiet outside of a Selah moment, that peace is being spent for free where it should be reserved.

## Perceptual Register Pairs

These govern how the player should *look at and read* the things in front of them — a different kind of instruction than emotional register, aimed most directly at character, creature, and environment art.

### Specific Decay, Not Generic Grime

Per the Environment Art Direction doctrine — damage and wear should always read as a *particular* story (this wall failed because of this specific event, this Accursed carries this specific inversion), never as generic "old and dirty" texture-pack set dressing. **Drift signal:** if an environment or character's wear could be copy-pasted onto an unrelated game with no loss of meaning, it has failed this pair.

### Diminishment, Not Accumulation

The inversion principle states the Bleed does not add anything monstrous to a person — it *subtracts* what they refused to be, leaving them as an absence rather than an addition. Most monster design in the genre defaults to the opposite instinct: claws, fangs, extra limbs, glowing eyes layered on top as accumulation. Vigil's Accursed should read as something *taken away*, not something *added on* — even where transformation is visually dramatic (see Shaped, below), the underlying cause must remain subtractive, never additive, to stay consistent with the inversion principle. **Drift signal:** if a creature design's process started with "what cool monster features can we add," that's the wrong starting question for this game, every time.

## The Humanity Spectrum: "Human-Like, Until Nothing Remains"

This section governs the full range of Accursed visual presentation across the entire hierarchy, and resolves how tier and Shaped archetype relate to each other visually. It is stated separately from the paired contrasts above because it does more structural work than a simple pairing — it's a spectrum with a branch, not a single axis.

**The core rule:** Accursed presentation exists on a spectrum of humanity being stripped away. Where a given Accursed sits on that spectrum — and whether their body can still hold human shape at all — should always be legible from appearance alone.

### Tier as Depth of Extraction (how far along)

- **Thrall** — form fully intact, nature subtracted, extraction barely begun. What's missing is primarily behavioral (the "forgetting" fight pattern). A minority of Thralls may show a subtle visual tell hinting at a latent Shaped direction their transformation is straining toward — patchy fur beginning to show on a Feral-leaning Thrall, canines just slightly too long on a Predatory-leaning one, an unnerving stillness on a Wraith-leaning one. This is never a full transformation, never grotesque, and never mechanically different in combat — Thralls cannot be fully Shaped (see Shaped-Tier Relationship below); the tell is a fever before the illness, not the illness itself. Most Thralls should remain undifferentiated — a Thrall whose specific trait-loss never crystallized toward any particular direction is equally valid, and should likely remain the majority. Tell-bearing Thralls are a meaningful minority that rewards player attention, the same way the vital point shimmer rewards attention rather than being handed over outright. This detail exists specifically to keep the majority of early, frequent combat (Thralls being the most commonly fought tier) from reading as undifferentiated horror-mass, which would work against "The Accursed are never monsters" by giving the player nothing on the surface to anchor a specific person to, especially before the Selah moment's reframe has a chance to land.

### The Shaped-Tier Relationship

Shaped archetypes and Accursed tier are two related but distinct axes, not fully independent:

- **Tier** governs depth of extraction, vital point behavior, and combat cognition (forgetting vs. collecting vs. aware-and-indifferent).
- **Shaped** governs fighting style, color signature, and which specific virtue was inverted.
- **Shaped can only fully manifest at Retained or Lucid tier.** A specific trait-inversion requires enough surviving will and enough time for one trait to become the thing that breaks, rather than everything breaking at once — which is why Thrall (too early, too undifferentiated) and Hollow (never human to begin with) can never be fully Shaped. Thrall-tier tells (above) are the sole exception, existing as pre-echoes rather than a contradiction of this rule.
- **Not every Retained or Lucid is Shaped.** A plain Retained or Lucid with no Shaped archetype represents someone whose inversion never crystallized around one specific trait — narratively distinct from, not lesser than, a Shaped counterpart.
- **Ancient/Nosferatu is a duration modifier, not a third axis** — any Shaped who has continued extraction well past initial transformation, growing more dangerous and more total in their inversion over time. Not a separate tier or archetype slot.

**On Ancients specifically — a deliberate design note on scarcity and presence-through-absence.** Ancients are conceived as potentially approaching, but still a step below, Hollow-level danger — and are intended to remain extremely rare, reserved for high-level content or specific story beats requiring that scale of threat. For the foreseeable term, Ancients should exist primarily as **rumor and whisper** rather than confirmed, encountered content — oral history among Hunters, unconfirmed accounts, something older than any known Lucid, potentially predating the current accelerated Bleed entirely (consistent with the deep lore's note that essence collection occurred slowly for millennia before the Accord's current terms intensified it). This is a deliberate use of absence as atmospheric tool: the implication that something like this exists, without ever needing to build or show it, does real narrative work at effectively zero production cost, and should be exploited through incidental dialogue and world-texture rather than rushed into mechanical existence.
- **Retained** — form intact, nature subtracted further. The poke-through moments should read as brief, visible flickers of the human returning to face and body language — not additions, returns.
- **Lucid** — form fully intact, nature's absence total and complete. This is the spectrum's most disturbing point specifically *because* nothing external shows it — the horror is invisibility, not transformation.
- **Hollow** — outside the human spectrum entirely; see Environment Art Direction's scale-discontinuity doctrine for the visual language governing Hollow-adjacent content.

### Shaped as a Parallel Branch (not a further point on the same line)

**Shaped is not "worse than Lucid."** It answers a different question. Where tier measures *how far along* extraction has progressed, Shaped measures *how total* the extraction has become in one specific dimension of a person's nature — generosity, discipline, outward connection, individual identity. When subtraction in one dimension becomes total enough, the body itself can no longer hold human shape in that dimension, and something new fills the vacancy left behind. This is why a Shaped archetype can exist at Retained-tier (as with Eagle's Landing's Feral mini-boss) — tier and Shaped-ness are independent axes answering different questions, not a single ladder.

**Critically, this transformation remains subtractive, not additive, even though it is visually dramatic:**
- The **Predatory** does not gain new monstrous hunger — they lose whatever inhibited pure taking, and the body's presentation shifts to reflect a creature that only consumes.
- The **Feral** does not gain animal traits — they lose the containment that kept a body looking civilized, and what's revealed was always latent underneath, always restrained until now.
- The **Wraith** does not gain a haunting presence — they lose everything external, and what's left is pure, involuntary interiority projected outward because nothing else remains to hold it in.
- The **Assembled** does not gain mass as an addition — they lose the individuality that once made them impossible to overlook. Assembled emerged from people who were most outwardly confident and audaciously themselves — flamboyant, self-assured, unequivocally individual. The Bleed stripped that self-possession away, and in its absence the fear of being noticed, of being singular and exposed, drives them to merge with others out of a desperate need to disappear. Critically, this is not a single completed loss the way Feral, Predatory, and Wraith each represent — it is an *ongoing* failure. The hiding never succeeds. The seams remain visible, each one evidence that the merging did not actually erase anyone — it only trapped them mid-vanishing, still individually recognizable, still exactly as impossible to overlook as they always were. The tragedy made visible in an Assembled's body is the failure of the hiding itself: people who could not stand to be diminished, now forced into the one shape more impossible to ignore than the one they feared.

Every Shaped transformation, however visually extreme, must be traceable to a specific *loss*, never framed as a gain. This keeps the archetype consistent with Diminishment, Not Accumulation above, even at its most visually transformed extreme.

## How to Use This Document

For any new art, audio, animation, or VFX work: check it against the relevant pairs before finalizing, not after. This document is meant to catch drift at the concepting stage, when it's cheap to correct, not at review, when it's expensive to redo.

This is a **living document.** New pairs should be added whenever a new system or story beat implies a sensory register that isn't yet captured above — the working method (trace it back to something already locked, state it as a contrast against a plausible wrong target) applies to any future addition the same way it applied to everything currently in this document.

## Design Tenet Alignment

- **Every system serves the feeling** — this document exists specifically to make sure art and audio disciplines, which don't have the luxury of a tooltip to fall back on, are serving that same feeling as rigorously as every mechanical system already does.
- **The Accursed are never monsters** — the entire Humanity Spectrum section is a direct visual enforcement of this tenet, extended into a rule character art can actually be built and checked against.
- **Weight before spectacle** — nearly every pair in this document is, in some form, choosing restraint and specificity over genre-default spectacle (no fanfare, no ambient calm outside Selah, no accumulation-based monster design).
